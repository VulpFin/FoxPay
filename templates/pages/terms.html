1. What Fox Pay is, in legal terms

Fox Pay is a technical service that routes a payment to a payment provider. It is not a bank, a money transmitter, an acquirer, a custodian or the seller of anything you buy. The merchant is the seller and the merchant of record; the provider (Stripe, PayPal, Square or the crypto network) moves the money.

2. If you are paying for something

  • Your contract for the goods or services is with the merchant, not with us. Delivery, quality, cancellations and refunds are theirs to answer for.
  • Refunds start with the merchant. We can only return money the merchant instructs us to return, through the provider that took it.
  • Card details are entered with the provider, not with us. We never see or store a card number.
  • The amount shown at checkout is what is authorised. Your bank or wallet may apply its own fees or currency conversion.
  • An authorisation is not a payment: it places a hold that is either captured or released. A crypto invoice settles when the network confirms it, and an underpaid or expired invoice may need the merchant's help to resolve.
  • Disputes and chargebacks go through your card issuer or wallet provider, and we cooperate with whatever they require.
