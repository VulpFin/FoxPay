# FoxPay Merchant Agreement (Draft for Attorney Review)

Version: draft-2026-09-14

This is an operational draft, not legal advice and not an approved production agreement. TG11 LLC should have qualified counsel review it before public seller signup is enabled.

## Parties and service

The agreement is between TG11 LLC (operator of FoxPay) and the seller identified in the application. The seller is the merchant of record for its sales and is responsible for its goods, services, customer disclosures, delivery, and support. FoxPay supplies software that routes payment requests to the seller's chosen provider account. FoxPay does not collect card numbers, hold seller funds, maintain a seller payout balance, or pay sellers out. Provider settlement and payouts are governed by the provider's own terms.

## Provider accounts and authorization

The seller must maintain an authorized account with each connected provider, accept that provider's terms, and meet its eligibility, identity, sanctions, and prohibited-business requirements. The seller authorizes FoxPay to perform the payment, refund, and status actions supported by the connection it grants. The seller is responsible for the accuracy of its provider account and payout settings. FoxPay can suspend routing, revoke FoxPay API keys, or disconnect its provider access; it cannot pause a provider payout that it does not control.

## Fees and taxes

Provider processing, FX, refund, dispute, and other fees are determined by provider agreements. FoxPay application fees, if introduced, must be separately disclosed and accepted before activation. The default application fee is zero. Any future fee schedule must state whether fees are returned on full or partial refunds. The seller remains responsible for pricing, sales and other taxes, records, tax reporting, and any information returns applicable to its business. This agreement does not establish an exemption from any tax or reporting duty.

## Refunds, disputes, and negative balances

The seller owns its refund policy, customer disputes, chargebacks, evidence, and resulting provider negative balances. FoxPay may transmit authorized refund instructions to a provider, but the provider determines execution and fund movement. The seller must answer disputes and maintain enough funds or other arrangements required by the provider. FoxPay may restrict routing during fraud, unresolved disputes, provider restriction, or legal risk.

## Security and data

The seller must protect its FoxPay keys, webhook secrets, accounts, and authorized users; promptly report compromise; and use provider-hosted checkout for card data. The seller must not submit card numbers, CVV, magnetic-stripe data, or EMV data to FoxPay. FoxPay processes customer and transaction metadata to provide the service and investigate abuse. Privacy, retention, deletion, and subprocessors require review in the final agreement and privacy notice. Both parties must comply with applicable data-protection law.

## Acceptable use and termination

The FoxPay acceptable-use policy and provider rules apply. FoxPay may reject an application, suspend or terminate routing, revoke FoxPay credentials, or disconnect provider access for violations, security risk, provider instruction, or legal requirements, subject to final notice and appeal terms approved by counsel. The seller can stop using FoxPay and disconnect a provider, subject to records, pending disputes, and legal retention duties.

## Responsibility and legal terms for counsel

Counsel must finalize warranties, indemnity, liability limits, governing law, venue, dispute resolution, notices, TG11 LLC's legal address and contact details, effective date, amendment process, material-change notice, and reacceptance. None of those terms should be inferred from this draft. Counsel and accounting advisers must assess applicable payment, PCI, money-transmission, sanctions, consumer-protection, tax, and reporting obligations based on the real operating model and jurisdictions.
